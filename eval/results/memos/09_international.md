## Out of scope

I can't screen Dublin, Ireland or Frankfurt, Germany. My data covers only the contiguous US and DC, so I can't compare sites in other countries.

I can screen US counties for a hyperscale campus. You could send either of these:
- **A list of candidate counties.** Add the state if a name appears in several states. For example, "Loudoun, VA" and "Prince William, VA."
- **An open question.** For example, "Where should we look in Texas?" I'd rank the top candidates and profile them.

For each county, the memo covers:
- model score
- industrial power price
- grid queue
- existing and planned data centers
- risks
- next diligence steps

Send me a list of counties or a state to start.
